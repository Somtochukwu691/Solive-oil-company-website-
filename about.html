Our Story

Built on purity, grown on trust.

Solive Oil started with a simple idea: Nigerian kitchens deserve cooking oil that's genuinely clean, genuinely affordable, and made close to home.

Mission

Leading Nigeria's premium cooking oil sector, honestly.

From our facility in Nsukka, Enugu State, we set out to become one of the clear leaders in Nigeria's cooking oil sector — not by cutting corners, but by triple-filtering every batch, keeping our process transparent, and pricing our oil so it's within reach of every household, not just a few.

Every bottle of Solive Oil that leaves our facility carries a tamper-evident seal, a promise of consistency, and the same standard we'd want in our own kitchens.

From refinery to shelf, quality never leaves the process.
How We Got Here

Our journey so far

The Beginning

A refinery with a purpose

Solive Oil was founded with a clear mandate: produce cooking oil that Nigerian families could trust — triple filtered, cholesterol-free, and made without compromise.

Building the Standard

Investing in modern filtration

We built our production process around consistent, repeatable quality — modern filtration standards designed to remove impurities while preserving a clean, neutral taste in every bottle.

Reaching More Kitchens

Growing across Enugu State and beyond

What started as a local commitment to quality has grown into a brand recognized across the region, as more households and distributors choose Solive Oil for everyday cooking.

Today

Working toward category leadership

We continue to invest in our facility, our people, and our process — with the goal of being the clear leader in Nigeria's premium cooking oil sector, one satisfied customer at a time.
