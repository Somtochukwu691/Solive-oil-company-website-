Recipes made with Solive Oil

A neutral, clean-tasting oil that gets out of the way and lets Nigerian flavours lead. Here's what we're cooking.

Rice · 35 min

Classic Jollof Fried Rice

Parboiled rice, stir-fried with peppers, onions, and a splash of Solive Oil for a light, even fry that doesn't overpower the pepper base.

Snacks · 20 min

Rice Pancake (Rice Fritters)

A crispy, golden favourite — leftover rice bound with egg and pan-fried in Solive Oil until the edges turn crisp and lacy.

Breakfast · 15 min

Yam and Egg Sauce

Boiled yam paired with a pepper-and-egg sauce, sautéed in Solive Oil for a clean finish that lets the tomato and pepper shine.

Sides · 25 min

Golden Fried Plantain (Dodo)

Ripe plantain slices, shallow-fried in Solive Oil until deep gold on the outside and soft within — no burnt edges, no heavy aftertaste.

Mains · 40 min

Efo Riro (Vegetable Stew)

A rich, palm-and-pepper-forward vegetable stew — Solive Oil helps carry the spice blend evenly through every spoonful.

Street Food · 30 min

Puff-Puff

Soft, sweet, deep-fried dough balls — Solive Oil's clean taste keeps them tasting of sugar and yeast, not oil.
