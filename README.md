Solive Oil Company Website

A modern, responsive, and user-friendly corporate website for Solive Oil, designed to showcase premium cooking oil products, educate customers, and support distributors and retailers.

---

📌 Project Overview

The Solive Oil Company Website serves as the company's digital presence, allowing customers to explore products, learn about the brand, discover recipes, and contact the company. The platform is designed with performance, accessibility, and mobile responsiveness in mind.

---

✨ Features

Public Website

- Responsive homepage
- Company profile (About Us)
- Product catalogue
- Individual product pages
- Recipe & cooking tips section
- Health & nutrition information
- Blog & news
- Contact page with inquiry form
- Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
- Store locator
- Distributor registration
- Careers page
- Sustainability section
- Image gallery
- Social media integration

- Newsletter subscription
